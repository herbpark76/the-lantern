## In a Meeting

I declined the call and typed *In a meeting*, which is what I typed to everyone, and sent it.

Three dots appeared at once.

My father did not text. In my experience he regarded texting the way he regarded ketchup, as something that existed for other people and was not to be mentioned. He communicated by telephone, by letter, and through his secretary, in that order of preference. The dots went away, and came back, and went away.

*At 6:52?*

Then, a moment later:

*Your Indonesian business is on the wire. Deal with it before the Americans wake up.*

Then nothing for almost a minute. I stood in the hall in my pajamas and watched the screen. Down the hall Gabriella turned off the shower.

*Composure.*

He had learned to text. Someone had taught him, sitting beside him, probably, holding the phone at the right distance from his face and showing him where the letters were. It had taken patience. It had taken an afternoon. It had not been me.
