In the Zurich version of Frank's life he runs GAASCORE instead of Gaas Dynamics. It's in a former bank building on the Bahnhofstrasse, with the name in brushed nickel over the door. It turns everything a person has done with money into one number from 300 to 1000, used by banks, landlords, employers, insurers and two governments; below 600, doors get heavier. The number sits on everyone's phone lock screen, which was Frank's idea. Frank's score falls all day for no reason anyone can find: 912 → 909 → (931 if he defends the model) → 896 → 880 → 742 → 699. Hal Brenner is co-founder (name on the door in smaller letters); Sunny Rao runs Risk (in Los Angeles Frank fired Sunny in 2019); Ruth reads the numbers at Morning Ops. The Jakarta crisis: 40,000 motorbike-taxi drivers downgraded below 600 overnight, all of whom made the same 60,000-rupiah fuel payment, most at 2:51 a.m. (the time of the port attack in F). The model decided they were one person.
