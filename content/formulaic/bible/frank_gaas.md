Narrator. 43, worth eleven billion dollars, CEO of Gaas Dynamics. Wakes at 6:15 (the St. Albert's bell), sleeps five hours, walks his glass house in Bel Air "on marks." Family money from drainage pipe; his father's "Composure, Frank" runs in his head. Folds wrappers into tight squares. Drives a black armored G63. Married to Gabriella for eleven years.
