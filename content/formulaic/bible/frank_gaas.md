Narrator. 43, worth eleven billion dollars, CEO of Gaas Dynamics. Wakes at 6:15 (the St. Albert's bell), sleeps five hours, walks his glass house in Bel Air "on marks." Family money from drainage pipe; his father's "Composure, Frank" runs in his head. Folds wrappers into tight squares. Drives a black armored G63. Married to Gabriella for eleven years. His father, Malcolm Gaas, gave him the watch he wears every day (sent by courier on his eighteenth birthday). End of Chapter F: walking backward up Grand Avenue with his hands up, he hears his father's voice say "Check the cash, Frank," reaches for his breast pocket and is shot by a sniper. He narrates F from "somewhere else." In Chapter O he wakes in Zurich.
