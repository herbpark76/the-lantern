Kendra: Frank's assistant, 28, three years, the only one at the company who asks if he's okay (he doesn't answer). Ruth: reads the numbers at Morning Ops. Sunny Rao: engineer who built half the scheduling system, fired by Frank in 2019. Officer Alvarez: the officer who arrests Frank at the Grand Avenue surrender (moved from F to the end of R). In F the officer on the loudspeaker is unnamed. The pawnbroker on Vermont: eats sunflower seeds, gives Frank $1,900 and a yellow 30-day ticket for his father's watch. Mr. Ebb: Frank's driving instructor (glass eye). Pruitt: rang the St. Albert's bell, now sells boats in Florida. The man in the Suburban: forgettable face, works by anonymous text.
