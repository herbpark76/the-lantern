Frank's wife of eleven years. Was a cellist; Frank met her at a benefit he paid for, and she laughed at him. Her cello sits in a humidity-controlled music room nobody enters. Sits on three foundation boards, Pilates at eight, visits her sister Bea (lemons, dislikes Frank) in Ojai monthly. Her last real question, two years ago: "Are you happy?" Says "You're up early" with the same pause each morning.
