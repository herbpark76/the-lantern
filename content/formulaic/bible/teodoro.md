name: Teodoro
---

Frank's butler of six years. Brings coffee at 6:40 with the saucer handle at four o'clock. Keeps a small black notebook "manual" of Frank's preferences ("Handle at 4", "Will say 'fine' when it isn't"). Listens to a Portuguese talk station though he doesn't speak Portuguese. Buries the birds that hit the glass by the lemon trees.
