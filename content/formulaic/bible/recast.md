---

Reality keeps a finite cast and gives them new roles (the bible's Shadowplay idea). In Chapter O:
- Teodoro is the building concierge, with the same Portuguese radio.
- The gas station clerk is a tram inspector ("No ticket, hon?", "You want your receipt?").
- The pawnbroker is a Bahnhofstrasse jeweler with a duplicate of Malcolm's watch in the window.
- Hal is co-founder; at Frank's wedding he "wasn't there; you didn't invite me."
- Sunny Rao still works there and has a plant from Frank.
- Malcolm is alive in Geneva.
- Gabriella still plays the cello (Tonhalle, Elgar) and says "You're up early" with no pause.
- A Jakarta driver looks like Marcus/Desmond.
- The companion is Mia's patient: gone at 8:31, leaving a bomber jacket, the smell of apples, a banana peel and the pawn ticket.
- The coordinates card turns up on the back of Mia's business card.
Rule: one beat each, never explained.
