Main character (his own chapter is A). Black, late 50s, gray at the temples, good old overcoat with soft cuffs. Carries a small notebook of penciled numbers in neat columns, some circled. Calm, slightly hoarse voice; doesn't waste words. Mathematician and hacker who grew up amid instability, and finds numbers more dependable than people. First appears at the very end of Chapter O on the Tonhalle steps. He glances at Frank's GAASCORE number, makes a mark and says: "You're early this time … Usually it takes you a lot longer to notice the coffee." Gives only his first name. Planned: appears disguised as a janitor in U.
