Main character (her own chapter is M). Psychiatrist, early 30s. In Chapter O she works at a clinic on the Zürichberg. She has a dark coat with one mismatched button, hair coming loose from an overworked clip, tired, steady eyes, and drives a dented red Fiat with an empty child seat in the back. Says she doesn't believe "in this kind of thing." In O she sits down at Frank's lunch table as if they've met ("You said you'd come"). She slips: "I'm not here as your doctor," which plants the hospital in Part II. Her request: a young man on her ward, no papers, three names, asks for Frank every morning at 7:40 and says Frank owes him a lemon tree. She brings Frank the gas station receipt. If Frank refuses her: "That's what you said last time." Bible: raised in a loving, unconventional home; can hold two contradictions without forcing one to win.
