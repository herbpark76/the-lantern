Young man, about 26, cheap thrift-store bomber jacket, grin, smells of apples. Planted in the house glass, the neighbor's 11-ft wall, the lobby revolving door and the rearview mirror before he gets in at Sunset and Doheny. Nobody else reacts to him. Knows about the music room and speaks in Frank's father's voice ("Composure, Frank"), hinting he is Frank's projection. His name shifts each timeline. At downtown the seat belt is still buckled across nobody.
