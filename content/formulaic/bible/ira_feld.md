Frank's lawyer for nineteen years. Bills in six-minute increments, uses paper because it makes people nervous, doesn't know his doormen's names. Arranges a controlled surrender: "Two o'clock. My office... a process, not a story." Slips and calls Frank "Mr. G", which only Marcus does. Assistant: Denise.
