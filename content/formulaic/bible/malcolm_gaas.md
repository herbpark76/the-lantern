Frank's father. Family money from drainage pipe. Praised composure and appearances, not feelings; gave gifts instead of presence (the watch for Frank's eighteenth came by courier from Geneva, with a card signed by his secretary). Counted his change at restaurants, in front of the waiter, every time: "a man who does not check is a man who will be taken." Died at Cedars-Sinai in 2016 ("Is it raining?"). His voice ("Composure, Frank") runs in Frank's head, and the companion borrows it. At the end of Chapter F his voice says "Check the cash, Frank," and Frank's reach for his pocket gets him shot. Possible payoff: alive in Zurich in Chapter O.
