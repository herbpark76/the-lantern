# Formulaic — voice guide (author notes, not shown to readers)

- Frank narrates in past tense, from "somewhere else" (revealed at the end of downtown: "I am telling you this from somewhere else now. I will not say where yet.").
- Vonnegut voice: deadpan, very short titled chapters, refrains ("You're up early", "I would like that noted", "Composure, Frank").
- Philip K. Dick is the reality-slipping engine; Gibson for texture; Bradbury sparingly.
- Plotting from Headhunters: plant and payoff, chapter-end reversals, flaw-driven plot, the marriage as the hidden heart. Keep brand names minimal (the G63 stays; it's a plot object).
- Dialogue must sound like real people (Ira is a practical white-collar defense lawyer arranging a controlled surrender). Plant characters before they matter; no abrupt entrances.
- Never explain the mechanism in game or code language. Avoid stock thriller phrasing.
- Text-first. Only felt-tip drawings of in-world objects, never faces: the saucer, the place card, the coordinates card, the DESMOND name tag.
- Closing idea: "The choices don't change where you end up. They change who arrives."
- Timelines bleed: replays change small details — the saucer handle hour, the companion's name (Dex/Jax/Rex/Max/Lex), the MARCUS ghost under DESMOND.
